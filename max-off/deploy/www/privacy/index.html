MaxOff

Privacy policy

Last updated 16 September 2026

MaxOff is a Shopify app made by MPC Trades. It puts a maximum amount on a percentage discount — 15% off, but never more than 150.00. This page explains what the app stores, why it stores it, and how to have it removed.

The short version. MaxOff stores the discounts you create and the settings for your shop. It does not read your orders, it does not collect your customers' names, emails or addresses, and it adds nothing to your storefront. Uninstalling the app deletes your data.

What MaxOff stores

All of it belongs to your shop, and all of it is created by you using the app.

WhatWhy
Your shop's domain and an access token To identify your shop and talk to the Shopify Admin API on your behalf. The token is issued by Shopify and can be revoked by uninstalling.
Your capped discounts The code or title, the percentage, the maximum, the currency, the dates, usage limits and combination settings. This is the app's core function.
Your shop settings Currency, time zone, rounding preference, the wording shown at checkout, your plan, and the defaults used when you create a new discount.
A little interface state Whether you have dismissed the setup guide, and the figures from the last cart you tested, so the app can show you where you left off.

What MaxOff does not collect

  • No customer personal data. No names, email addresses, postal addresses, phone numbers or customer IDs — not for your customers, and not for their orders.
  • No order data. MaxOff does not request the read_orders permission and does not read your orders.
  • No payment details. Charges for the app are handled entirely by Shopify billing; MaxOff never sees a card.
  • No tracking. There are no analytics scripts, no advertising pixels, and no profiling of you or of your buyers.
  • Nothing in your storefront. MaxOff adds nothing to your theme. There is no script to install and no snippet to remove.

The permissions MaxOff asks for

Only these three, and nothing else:

  • write_discounts — to create, edit, pause and delete your capped discounts.
  • read_discounts — to read back the discounts it created, and to check a code is not already taken.
  • read_products — so the cart tester can show a product picker from your own catalogue. Product details are used to calculate a preview and are not stored.

How the discount is applied

The maximum is worked out by a Shopify Function running inside Shopify's own discount engine at checkout. The percentage and the maximum are stored on the discount itself, in a Shopify metafield. Nothing about a buyer is sent to MaxOff, and no cart or checkout data leaves Shopify.

Where your data is kept

On a private server operated by MPC Trades, in a database that is not reachable from the public internet. Traffic to the app is encrypted with TLS. MaxOff uses no third-party analytics, advertising or data-broker services, and does not sell or share your data with anyone.

The only other party involved is Shopify itself, which hosts your store and processes app charges.

How long it is kept, and how to delete it

  • Uninstall the app. Shopify notifies MaxOff and your session and access token are deleted immediately. Your access is revoked at the same moment.
  • Ask for full erasure. When Shopify sends a shop erasure request — normally 48 hours after an uninstall — every remaining row belonging to your shop is deleted: your discounts, your settings and any reporting rows.
  • Ask us directly. Email team@mpctrades.com at any time and we will delete your data and confirm when it is done.

MaxOff also answers Shopify's customer data request and customer erasure webhooks. Because the app holds no customer-linked data, there is nothing to export or erase in response to those — but the requests are received and handled rather than ignored.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, or to receive a copy of it. Write to team@mpctrades.com and we will respond within 30 days.

If this changes

If MaxOff ever needs a permission it does not have today — for example reading orders, so it can show you how much your maximums saved — this page will be updated first, the date at the top will change, and Shopify will ask you to approve the new permission before it takes effect. A new permission cannot be granted without your explicit approval.
